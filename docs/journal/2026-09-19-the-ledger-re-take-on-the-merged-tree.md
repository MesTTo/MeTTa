# The ledger re-take on the merged tree
Goal: every benchmark ledger (extensions/python/benchmarks/{baseline,cost-baseline,scaling-baseline,memory-scale-baseline,extension-baseline}.json, engine/bench-baseline.json, extensions/cmetta/benchmarks/baseline.json, extensions/mork/benchmarks/baseline.json) pinned to the merged tree, every moved row carrying prose that places its move on the commits that made it, so the eleven ledger lanes are green on the tip the trunk fast-forwards to.
Constraint: a moved row without a placed step is reported, never pinned (ai-tmp/ai_compose_ledger.py refuses it); the ledgers keep a hand order the update doors would destroy, so numbers go in row-locally; the user accepts small cost differences from the merge (2026-09-18) but a move of a class, not a constant, is root-caused first.
Plan:
1. Base: the branch's own re-take on d761b9c43, preserved as commit object afb8a7d74 (its baseline.json rows placed by a 27-point ladder), restored into the editing tree. Established by reading its prose.
2. Three fixed points per row: afb8a7d74 (d761b9c43), the merge parent 89bd5dc41, the tip 4c0533704; each lane measured at the last two (wt-battery-4 and -5). A row's move splits into K = d761b9c43..89bd5dc41 (the branch's commits) and M = the merge with its batch fix. Established by measurement.
3. K is placed by a ladder over the branch's engine-touching commits (d761b9c43 e59104ace fd0af38f7 55368cb4e 0d0455d22 12609a929 7cc8fb863 a9e2c06d3 f06186a96 49478d67a 89bd5dc41) restricted to the moved rows (ai_ledger_ladder.sh, ai_seat_ladder.sh, ai_costs_ladder.sh); M is one step whose mechanism the class_values profile names (ai-merge-mechanism.txt). Established by measurement.
4. Any move that is a class rather than a constant is bisected to its commit and root-caused before it is pinned. Discharged for query-where and register-op below.
5. Composition: ai_compose_ledger.py for the record ledgers, ai_repin_rows.py and ai_top_comment.py for the hand-ordered ones; then the lanes on the composed tree, then the commit and its pin.
