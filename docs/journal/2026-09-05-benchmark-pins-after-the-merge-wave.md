Goal: find out which committed benchmark pins the seven merges between e7cc36d2 and a94f804c moved, attribute each move to the merge that caused it, and re-pin only the ones whose cause is settled.
Constraint: inferences decide; the working checkout is shared with an integrator and must not be checked out or branched; a moved number is a finding until it is attributed, and an unattributed move is not re-pinned.
