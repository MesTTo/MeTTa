; grammar and the generated Pygments lexer could disagree without anyone
; noticing, each one a place the two engines' rules are not obviously the same:
; the whitespace and digit classes, where Oniguruma follows Unicode and

; One probe per `>>>>` line. tests/checks/check_tokenisation_parity.py writes
; each into a file of its own, tokenises it both ways, and compares every
; character; it also runs one of them again with CRLF endings. Nothing here is
; a runnable program, and nothing runs it.
